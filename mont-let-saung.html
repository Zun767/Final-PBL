Ingredients

  • Mont-let-saung jelly
  • Coconut milk: 1 cup
  • Palm sugar or jaggery: 1/2 cup (or to taste)
  • Water: 2 cups
  • Salt: A pinch
  • Pandan leaves: 1 or 2
  • Sesame seeds or grated coconut (for garnish)

Steps By Step

1.In a pot, combine 1 cup of coconut milk, 2 cups of water, and 1/2 cup of palm sugar or jaggery, and heat until the sugar dissolves.

2.Add a pinch of salt and pandan leaves.

3.Mix with mont-let-saung jelly/

4.Garnish with sesame seeds or grated coconut and serve warm or at room temperature
