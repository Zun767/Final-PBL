Ingredients

  • Agar-agar powder – 1 packet (usually about 7-10 grams)
  • Coconut milk – 1 can (about 400 ml)
  • Water – 2-3 cups (depending on how firm you want the jelly to be)
  • Sugar – ½ to ¾ cup (adjust to taste)
  • Salt – a pinch (to balance the sweetness)

Steps By Step

1.In a small pot, combine 1½ cups of water, 1 teaspoon of agar-agar powder, and 3 tablespoons of sugar. Stir well and bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring constantly to dissolve the agar-agar completely.

2.If you’d like to add pandan flavor or green color, add a few drops of pandan extract to the mixture, then turn off the heat.

3.Pour this clear layer mixture into a shallow dish or mold and let it cool until it begins to set slightly but is still tacky on top.

4.In another pot, combine 1½ cups of water, 1 teaspoon of agar-agar powder, 3 tablespoons of sugar, and a pinch of salt. Stir well and bring to a boil over medium heat, ensuring the agar-agar and sugar dissolve completely.

5.Lower the heat and stir in ½ cup of coconut milk, letting it simmer gently for 1-2 minutes without boiling vigorously. Turn off the heat.

6.Once the clear layer has set slightly, gently pour the coconut mixture over the top.

7.Let the dessert cool at room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 2-3 hours until fully set.

8.Slice the Kyauk Kyaw into squares or diamond shapes and serve chilled.
