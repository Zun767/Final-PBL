Ingredients

  • Fermented tea leaves: 1 cup
  • Fried garlic: 2 tbsp
  • Roasted peanuts: 1/4 cup
  • Roasted sesame seeds: 1/4 cup
  • Fried chickpeas or split peas: 1/4 cup
  • Shredded cabbage or lettuce: 1 cup
  • Diced tomato: 1 medium
  • Grated carrot: 1/2 cup
  • Green chilies: 2, thinly sliced
  • Minced garlic: 1–2 cloves
  • Lime juice: 2 tbsp
  • Fish sauce: 2 tbsp
  • Vegetable oil: 2 tbsp
  • Chili flakes: 1 tsp

Steps By Step

1.Rinse and prepare fermented tea leaves or grind fresh ones into a paste with oil, lime juice, and garlic.

2.Fry thinly sliced garlic until golden and set aside.

3.Roast peanuts, sesame seeds, and chickpeas until crispy.

4.Mix tea leaves with lime juice, fish sauce (or soy sauce), minced garlic, and chili flakes.

5.Toss in shredded cabbage, sliced tomatoes, grated carrots, and crunchy toppings.

6.Serve mixed or arranged in separate piles for individual mixing.
