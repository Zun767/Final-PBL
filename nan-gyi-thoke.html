Ingredients

  • Rice noodles (thick variety): 200g
  • Chicken (boneless): 1 cup, cooked and shredded (alternatively, use cooked chicken breast)
  • Chickpea flour: 1 tbsp
  • Lime juice: 2 tbsp
  • Fish sauce: 1 tbsp
  • Chili flakes: 1 tsp (optional, for spice)
  • Sesame oil: 1 tbsp
  • Coriander leaves: A handful, chopped (for garnish)
  • Fried onions: 2 tbsp (for garnish)
  • Boiled eggs: 2, halved (for garnish)
  • Coriander powder: 1 tsp (optional)

Steps By Step

1.Cook the rice noodles according to package instructions, then drain and rinse them under cold water.

2.Shred the cooked chicken into small pieces.

3.In a small pan, heat sesame oil, then sauté minced garlic and ginger until fragrant.

4.Add chickpea flour to the pan and fry until golden brown, then stir in coconut milk, lime juice, fish sauce, and chili flakes

Cook the mixture for a few more minutes until it thickens slightly.

In a large bowl, toss the cooked noodles with shredded chicken, then pour the dressing over and mix well.

Garnish with fried onions, boiled egg halves, sliced cucumber, and chopped coriander leaves.
